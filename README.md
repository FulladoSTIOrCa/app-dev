### Why I love the show naruto
The show naruto portrays many themes all throughout the series.
But has a big theme on comradery, friendship. and deep bonds.

Something I like about the show as well is the well choreographed fights and animation.
Mostly during fillers and small story development. the animation is often simple and quite choppy.
But when it comes to the intese ninja fights that the naruto series shows. The animators Lock in,
and animates them in such fluid and smooth motion. Where the fights are often greatly paced.

Something to take away from the show itself is the Character development in the anime.
In *Naruto* the Main character "Naruto" is seen early in the story as a chaotic kid with not much affinity in ninjutsu.
He often creates chaos by tagging the village with his graffiti and causing minor mischeif.

But in *Naruto: Shippuden* The main cast Grows in different directions due to the Major conflict that happened back in
*Naruto* The main charater "Naruto" is more mature and deals with situations more rationally than his younger counterpart.
